Beeldpakket

Everything needed to generate the seven artwork assets and get them into the game. Each prompt below is complete on its own — format, style, scene and exclusions in one block — so one copy is one paste, with nothing to assemble first and no size to remember to set.

STAP 1

Generate

Copy a prompt, paste it into the image generator, set the size shown on the card. Make three or four, pick one.

STAP 2

Crop & name

Crop, resize, save as WebP under 120 KB. The filename is what routes it — get it exactly right.

STAP 3

Judge in the game

Drag it onto proefstudio.html. Check it at phone size, with the scrim on, while scrolling. Then keep or regenerate.

The style, in one screen

Already written into every prompt. This is here so you can judge against it.

Constant

This is what makes seven separate generations look like one game.

  • The light — warm amber, low, coming from the stage toward the viewer.
  • Bright at the top, deep and quiet at the bottom. That is how a lit stage looks, and it hands us the legibility for free.
  • Five to eight big rounded shapes. Detail lives in the light, not the objects.
  • Crowds are flat silhouettes — rounded heads, no faces.
  • Never any text, letters, numbers, signage or logos.
  • Amber appears as light, never as gold objects. Gold and cyan belong to the buttons.

Variable

Only one thing changes between scenes: the colour of the room. Lock everything else.

A single palette for all three venues would make arriving at the stadium — the payoff of the whole rank ladder — feel like nothing happened. It would also sit oddly beside the twelve bright podiums the game already sells.

club — deep violet theatre — aubergine stadium — midnight blue

Same depth of value in all three — the room must stay darker than the answer tiles. It is a brightness rule, not a colour rule.

After generating

Crop, shrink, convert — then judge it where it will actually live.

Without a terminal

The straightforward route.

  • Crop and resize to the card's Deliver size in any image editor, or in squoosh.app, which does both in the browser.
  • In Squoosh choose WebP, set quality to about 80, and watch the file-size readout until it is under 120 KB.
  • Rename to exactly the filename on the card.

With a terminal

Same result, one line.

cwebp -q 80 -resize 1200 700 \
  in.png -o venue-club.webp

Then check the size: anything over 120 KB, drop the quality to 72 and try again before touching the dimensions.

When it comes back nearly right

Send one of these as a follow-up rather than starting over. Regenerate from scratch only if two of them in a row do not land it.

What you gotSay this
A square imageRegenerate this as a wide landscape image, 1536 by 1024 pixels. Same scene, same colours, nothing else changed.
Letters, a sign or a logo crept inRemove every letter, word, number, sign and logo from the image. Any surface that had writing on it becomes plain and smooth.
Too fussy — dies at thumbnail sizeSimplify it hard. Use half as many shapes, each one bigger and rounder, and put the richness into the lighting instead of into objects. It must still read clearly at the size of a phone thumbnail.
The bottom is busy or brightMake the lower half of the image much darker, smoother and emptier — an unbroken calm field with no shapes, edges or highlights. Keep everything interesting in the top half.
The crowd has facesReplace every face with a smooth featureless oval. The crowd is one flat silhouette mass of rounded heads and shoulders, with no features at all.
Gold-looking objects appearedRemove all gold and brass objects. Warm amber may only appear as light and glow, never as a gold-coloured thing.
map-sky: the black is not blackMake the background pure solid black, hex #000000, everywhere the clouds are not — a completely flat even black field, no gradient, no vignette, no dark blue.
The room is the wrong colourKeep the composition exactly as it is and change only the colour of the room to [deep violet / warm aubergine / midnight blue], at the same darkness.
